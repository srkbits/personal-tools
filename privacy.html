Privacy policy

These tools are used only by their owner, on the owner's Google account. They collect nothing from anyone else.

Calendar data read from Google is kept on the owner's computer. Backups stored in the owner's Google Drive are encrypted before they leave that computer, and the tools can reach only the Drive files they create.

The data is used only to help the owner. It isn't sold or used for advertising. Parts of it may be sent to an AI service the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude) to do that work. It goes to nobody else.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The owner can remove this access at any time from the owner's Google account.
